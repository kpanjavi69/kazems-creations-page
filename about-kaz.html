The Person Behind the Ecosystem

Kazem Panjavi

Olympian · Coach · Author · Technical Official

Barcelona ‘92 Olympian 7× Gold Medalist National Record Holder GB National Team & Odisha Chief Coach

Every product in the KAZ ecosystem exists because of a problem Kazem Panjavi met on the platform first — as a lifter, then as a coach, then as the official holding the clock. Nothing here was designed from theory.

The Athlete

Kazem Panjavi competed at the 1992 Barcelona Olympic Games. Across his competitive career he took seven gold medals and held a national record. That period gave him the thing no textbook can hand over: direct experience of what happens to technique, timing and judgement when a lift actually counts.

The Coach

Over 35 years of coaching, he has worked with more than 1,300 athletes — from a first session with an empty bar through to international competition. He has served as a Great Britain national team coach and as Chief Coach at the Odisha High Performance Centre, and he coaches at Ealing Weightlifting Club in West London.

The Official

As an international technical official, he has judged from the other side of the platform — the seat that decides whether a lift is three white lights or a red one. That perspective runs directly through Six Attempts: Six White Lights and through the referee logic built into Kaz Competition IQ.

The Organiser

Between 2014 and 2018 he staged four editions of the Phoenix Cup, organised single-handed with no national governing body support. Three reached the official IWF calendar, drawing national teams and clubs from seven countries: Germany, Belgium and Sweden at national team level, plus two teams from France, and entries from Austria, Switzerland and India. The Fantastic Four is its successor — a permanent recurring team event where lifters of different ages, genders and strengths score as one team.

The Creator

Three books, one mobile application, one competition software platform and one hardware feedback system — each built to close a gap he had personally run into. The books carry the knowledge, KAZAPP carries the daily programming, Competition IQ carries the match-day rehearsal, and the Kaz Path System carries the instant technical feedback on the bar.
